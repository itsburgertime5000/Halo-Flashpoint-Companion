    QUICK RULES

    Hill locations: A at D4–E5; B at G1–H2; C at A1–B2; D at G7–H8; E at A7–B8. Every hill occupies a 2×2 cube area including all stacked levels.

    Capture: A friendly model inside the hill, with no enemy model there, takes a free Auxiliary action to plant a flag. A newly placed flag replaces the opposing flag.

    Scoring: At round end, the team with its flag left in the active hill gains 5 VP, then removes the flag. Each enemy model killed is worth 1 VP. Add applicable Achievement and keyword VP manually.

    Duration: Up to 5 rounds, or until a team reaches 30 VP.

    Terrain: The central tower uses three containers (two adjacent, one stacked). Players alternate placing scatter terrain until all scenery is set.

    Husky King of the Hill Map

    Tap a cube in the active hill to plant or replace a flag using the selected fireteam. Each zone is 2×2; the board follows the supplied scenario map.
